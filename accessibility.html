Accessibility

Built to work for more people, by default.

Accessibility is treated as part of the interface, not a separate mode.

Our commitment

MediaMerge is designed toward WCAG 2.2 AA. That means content should be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for people using a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification, or reduced motion settings.

What this looks like in practice

  • Every interactive element can be reached and used from a keyboard alone.
  • Focus is always visible and never trapped.
  • Status is never communicated by color alone.
  • Motion respects your system's reduced-motion setting.
  • Captions and subtitle tracks are treated as first-class metadata, not an afterthought.

Tell us what isn't working

If something in the product is hard to use with assistive technology, that is a defect worth fixing. Reach out through the contact page and describe what you ran into.
