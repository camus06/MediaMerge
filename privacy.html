Privacy

What we collect, and why.

A short, direct account of how MediaMerge handles data. This is a portfolio project placeholder, not a binding legal document.

Account information

We keep the minimum needed to operate an account: name, email, workspace role, and subscription tier.

Usage data

Playback events are recorded against an asset to support royalty calculation and recommendations. This data is used internally and is not sold to third parties.

Rights and licensing data

Ownership and license terms are stored as entered by rights holders and are visible only to authorized workspace members.

Your choices

You can request an export or deletion of your account data at any time through contact.
